# Bio

My name is Derek Sheerer, and I am a Finance student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, expecting to graduate in Fall 2026. I am interested in entrepreneurship, business, and finding ways to combine those interests with the outdoor and surf industry.

Living in Hawaiʻi has strengthened my interest in the ocean and outdoor lifestyle and has influenced the types of businesses and projects that interest me. I enjoy learning about how brands are built, how businesses grow, and how financial decision-making can turn an idea into something sustainable.

My professional experience in hospitality has also taught me the importance of communication, teamwork, adaptability, and creating positive experiences for customers. As I continue developing my skills in finance and business, I hope to apply what I learn toward entrepreneurial opportunities and eventually build or contribute to businesses connected to the industries and lifestyle I care about.
