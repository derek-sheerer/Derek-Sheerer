# Executive Memo

**To:** Chief Financial Officer  
**From:** Derek Sheerer  
**Date:** July 31, 2026  
**Subject:** FX Receivable Exposure and Hedging Plan

Our company expects to receive **€4,500,000 in one year**, creating exposure to fluctuations in the EUR/USD exchange rate. While the euro amount is fixed, the value we receive in U.S. dollars is not. For example, if the exchange rate is **1.10 USD/EUR**, the payment will be worth **$4.95 million**. If the euro weakens to **1.00 USD/EUR**, the same payment falls to **$4.50 million**, reducing expected cash flow by **$450,000**. A move of this size is realistic within a normal year and could materially affect our budget, financial forecasts, and profitability.

There are three primary hedging approaches available to manage this risk. A **forward contract** locks in an exchange rate today for settlement when the payment is received. This provides certainty and protects our projected cash flow, but it also eliminates any benefit if the euro appreciates before settlement. A **money-market hedge** creates a similar fixed outcome by borrowing euros, converting them to U.S. dollars, and investing the proceeds until the payment date. This approach can be useful if forward contracts are unavailable or unattractive, but it requires borrowing capacity and is more operationally complex. Finally, a **currency put option** acts like insurance by guaranteeing a minimum exchange rate while allowing us to benefit if the euro strengthens. The trade-off is paying an upfront premium regardless of whether the option is ultimately exercised.

To support an informed decision, the next phase of this project will develop a structured hedging model. Phase 2 will define the workbook layout, formulas, assumptions, and validation checks. Phase 3 will use AI to build the model, followed by a thorough audit of its calculations. Phase 4 will replace placeholder values with current market data, and Phase 5 will independently validate the results before delivering a final recommendation on the most appropriate hedging strategy. This process provides a transparent, data-driven framework that supports confident financial decision-making.
